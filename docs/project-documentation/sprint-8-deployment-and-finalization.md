## Temperature Trend Insights

Implemented an executive analytics dashboard providing:

- Average temperature
- Highest temperature
- Lowest temperature
- Active alerts
- Temperature Risk Indicator
- Temperature Stability Score
- Operational Insight
- Recommended Actions

This provides warehouse managers with a concise operational overview of warehouse performance.

---

## AI Warehouse Risk Advisor

Implemented a rule-based operational decision-support dashboard capable of analysing warehouse conditions using:

- Temperature readings
- Inventory status
- Open alerts
- Warehouse Health Score
- Temperature Stability Score
- Operational Confidence
- Business Impact Assessment

The Operational Advisor dynamically evaluates warehouse conditions and provides:

- Operational Health Score
- Risk Level
- Risk Drivers
- Operational Insights
- Business Impact
- Recommendation Priority
- Assessment Summary

The solution was intentionally designed using a modular architecture to allow future integration of advanced Artificial Intelligence services, including Large Language Models (LLMs), without requiring significant architectural changes.

# Project Completion

Sprint 8 marks the successful completion of the ColdGuard capstone project.

ColdGuard demonstrates the practical application of modern software engineering principles through the implementation of a full-stack intelligent warehouse management solution incorporating Agile Scrum, RESTful API design, layered architecture, secure authentication, operational reporting, executive analytics, and rule-based operational decision support.

The completed project is supported by comprehensive sprint documentation, Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), automated testing, production deployment, and an end-to-end demonstration environment.

Future enhancements, including IoT integration, predictive analytics, machine learning, and conversational AI support, have been identified for continued development. However, the current implementation fully satisfies the objectives established at project inception and is ready for final academic assessment.
